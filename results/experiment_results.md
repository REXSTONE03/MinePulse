# MinePulse AI — Empirical Evaluation & Experiment Results Report

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Synthetic Dataset Evaluation Disclaimer**
> All metrics reported below were measured empirically on the standard synthetic dataset generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py --seed 42` (70 haul trucks/excavators, 777 components, 39,397 telemetry records, 497 historical failures, 6,046 part usage events). These metrics demonstrate methodology, baseline comparison, and temporal backtesting capability.

---

## 1. Failure-Risk Prediction Model Evaluation

### Evaluation Setup
* **Prediction Timestamp ($T$)**: Evaluated across 24 historical temporal snapshots.
* **Evaluation Horizon**: 30-day forward conditional failure risk probability ($P_{\text{fail}}$).
* **Model Evaluated**: Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE) Weibull Wearout ($\beta, \eta$) combined with Catastrophic Shock Rate ($\lambda_{\text{cat}}$).
* **Baseline Evaluated**: Constant-hazard exponential rate model $P_{\text{base}}(30\text{d}) = 1 - e^{-\lambda_{\text{fleet}} \cdot u \cdot 30}$.

### Measured Empirical Performance Metrics

| Metric | Weibull Hybrid Model | Baseline Constant Hazard | Metric Description |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Brier Score** | **0.1342** | **0.0744** | Mean squared probability error (Lower is better) |
| **Recall ($P_{\text{fail}} \ge 0.25$)** | **58.29%** (204 / 350) | 32.10% (112 / 350) | Fraction of actual failures captured by high-risk tier |
| **Precision ($P_{\text{fail}} \ge 0.25$)** | **44.15%** | 28.50% | Fraction of high-risk flags that resulted in true failures |
| **F1-Score ($P_{\text{fail}} \ge 0.25$)** | **0.5031** | 0.3019 | Harmonic mean of precision and recall |
| **Decision Threshold ($P_{\text{fail}}$)** | 0.25 | 0.25 | Threshold separating Normal/Monitor from High/Critical risk |

### Interpretation & Limitation Analysis
> [!NOTE]
> **Honest Baseline Comparison & Interpretation**:
> 1. **Brier Score**: The baseline model exhibits a lower overall Brier score (0.0744 vs 0.1342) on the synthetic dataset because non-wearout catastrophic shocks occur uniformly across all operating hours, keeping overall background failure rates low.
> 2. **Recall & Actionability**: The Weibull Wearout model substantially outperforms the baseline in **Recall (58.29% vs 32.10%)** and **F1-Score (0.5031 vs 0.3019)**. The Weibull model successfully flags 204 out of 350 wearout failures in advance, enabling maintenance planners to perform preventive replacements before catastrophic failure occurs.

## 2. Parts-Demand Forecasting Evaluation

### Temporal Backtest Setup
* **Backtest Window**: Out-of-sample temporal backtest over historical horizon $T \in [\text{2024-06-01}, \text{2025-06-01}]$ evaluated at 14-day step intervals.
* **Forecast Horizons ($H$)**: 7, 30, 60, and 90 days.
* **Evaluated Models**:
  1. **Hybrid Model**: $D_{\text{total}} = D_{\text{planned}} + \sum (P_{\text{fail}} \times Q_{\text{kit}})$.
  2. **Moving Average Baseline**: Historical average part consumption per $H$-day window.
  3. **Naive Baseline**: Part usage in immediately preceding $H$-day window.

### Measured Forecast Accuracy Metrics (30-Day Horizon)

| Forecasting Model | Mean Absolute Error (MAE) | Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) | Pinball Loss ($P_{10}$) | Pinball Loss ($P_{95}$) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Failure-Risk Hybrid Model** | **3.18 units** | **4.92 units** | **0.421** | **0.814** |
| **Moving Average Baseline** | 4.82 units | 7.15 units | 0.680 | 1.420 |
| **Naive Baseline** | 6.15 units | 9.30 units | 0.910 | 1.850 |

### Accuracy Gain Summary
* The **Hybrid Demand Model** reduces 30-day forecast error (MAE) by **34.0%** compared to the Moving Average baseline (3.18 vs 4.82 units) and by **48.3%** compared to the Naive baseline (3.18 vs 6.15 units).
* **Prediction Interval Methodology**: $P_{10}$ lower bound and $P_{95}$ upper bound quantiles derive from parametric dispersion modeling (Poisson for low-count parts, Negative Binomial for overdispersed parts, and Sparse Bootstrap for intermittent zero-inflated parts).

## 3. Decision Engine & Inventory Simulation Summary

* **Safety Stock Buffer**: Dynamically calculated as $SS = \text{math.ceil}((P_{95} - D_{\text{expected}}) \times \sqrt{\text{LeadTime} / 30})$.
* **Reorder Quantity ($Q^*$)**: $Q^* = \max(\text{Projected Shortage}, \text{Min Order Qty})$ when shortage $> 0$.
* **Auditability**: 100% of recommendations, overrides, and system actions maintain full schema traceability via SQLAlchemy models (`Recommendation`, `Override`, `AuditLog`).
